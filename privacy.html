VARG-Tools Privacy Notice

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Information used for sign-in

When Google OpenID Connect is enabled for the public hosted application, VARG-Tools will request only the openid and email scopes. The hosting service will use the email address returned by Google to identify the signed-in session. VARG-Tools will not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, or other Google API data.

Research files and analysis

Files uploaded through the application are processed in a dedicated application container for the active session. Containers share a server-mounted directory used for operational heavy-computation lock metadata; uploaded research files are not intentionally written to that shared directory. The reviewed application paths do not intentionally send uploaded research files to Google or another external analysis service, but this notice is not a guarantee that every software dependency has no network capability. Users can explicitly download project files and analysis outputs to their own device.

Do not upload personal, confidential, controlled, or otherwise sensitive information. The hosted service is provided for research data appropriate for processing on a public research application.

Operational records

The server and its hosting components may record operational information needed to run and protect the service, including the authenticated email identity, network and request metadata, login outcomes, session/container events, errors, and resource-limit events. A fixed retention period for these operational records has not been established, so this notice does not promise a specific deletion schedule.

Purpose and sharing

Identity and operational information are used to provide authenticated access, diagnose failures, enforce fair-use limits, and protect the research server. The project does not sell this information or use it for advertising.
