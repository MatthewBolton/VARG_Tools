VARG-Tools Terms of Use

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Research use

VARG-Tools is an open-source research application for tephra geochemical processing, visualization, correlation, and chronology workflows. Users remain responsible for reviewing analytical outputs and deciding whether they are suitable for a scientific interpretation or publication.

Data responsibility

Users must have the right to upload and process their data. Do not upload personal, confidential, controlled, unlawful, or malicious content. Save important work and outputs locally; hosted sessions are temporary computing sessions and may end because of inactivity, maintenance, or resource limits.

Fair use and service protection

Automated bulk access, attempts to evade login or resource limits, denial-of-service activity, and use that interferes with other researchers are prohibited. The service limits concurrent compute-intensive work and may reject or end operations to protect availability.

No warranty

The software is provided under the MIT License, without warranty of any kind. Availability of the hosted service is not guaranteed.

Citation and support

Please follow the citation guidance shown in VARG-Tools and cite the underlying reference datasets used in published work. Questions can be sent to bolton1+varg@ualberta.ca.
